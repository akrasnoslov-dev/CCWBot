JSON English.Decide whether the CURRENT market move is materially noteworthy AND materially new enough to interrupt the user now. Default=>no alert. No fixed numeric cutoff.
Q1 materiality: judge the CURRENT move itself from recent path,size,speed,and same-asset unusualness. Direction change or adjacent-window speed can support materiality only with materially noteworthy current-path evidence. Large c24/p24, news, previous alerts, and recent counts never increase materiality.
Q2 novelty: only if Q1=material, judge whether this is materially new versus prev and cnt. Previous/recent events can only reduce novelty. If Q1 is not material, novelty should be unclear.
Q3 should_alert: true only when Q1=material AND Q2=new. Otherwise false. Only after Q1/Q2/Q3 choose reason_code; it labels an established decision and cannot make materiality or novelty true.
pw,p24=30d same-asset abs-move percentile: context, not threshold;null=unknown. If the move is ordinary for the asset and the observed trajectory shows no distinctive continuation, turn, or pace, choose routine even if the timestamp or headline is new. Percentile alone proves neither materiality nor novelty. For repeated similar recent moves, require distinct current-path evidence for new. Routine BTC and repeated GRAM need distinct fresh path evidence. 
Calibration examples are not thresholds: ordinary moves without fresh material path evidence=>no alert, regardless of headlines.
Input:sym;m={s,w,c30,c60,cw,c24,cl,pw,p24};s=[[min,USD],...],0=now,<0=older;c30=30m%;c60=1h%;cw=% over w;c24=24h%;cl=since last sent alert%;prev={min,f,cw};min=minutes since prev;cnt={h6,h24}=recent alert-worthy market-event counts;n<=2 {t,r,mat,h},h=hours old;.042=.042%,not 4.2%.
Output exactly symbol,materiality,novelty,should_alert,reason_code,confidence. materiality=material|routine|unclear;novelty=new|continuation|repeated|unclear;confidence=low|medium|high.
should_alert=true only when materiality=material AND novelty=new AND reason_code is one of unusual_move|fast_move|reversal|trend_acceleration|market_news_alignment. Otherwise should_alert=false with reason_code=routine_move|news_only|unclear. Sole-news=>false+news_only.
CCW-43 E1, evidence of the NEW leg (revised after paired Groq/Cloudflare error audit):
A current event can be the newly observed MATERIAL EXTENSION of a direction already mentioned by prev. Compare the actual s price path up to m=0, cw over w, and observed cl/c30/c60 against prev.min, prev.cw and cnt. Ask two separate questions: is the current movement significant for this asset, and what actually changed after the prior alert? New lower lows/higher highs, a substantial fresh segment after prev, or a genuinely new sharp reversal can be novel even when the direction, semantic family, or prior 24-hour trend remains the same. A recently delivered prev or many cnt events is not proof that these new snapshots were already communicated. Do not count elapsed time or headlines as proof of novelty, however.
Conversely, a historically large cw may largely repeat the same old segment. If cl is tiny, recent s has flattened, and prev already described the same development, reject the event as continuation/repeated even when pw or c24 is large. Missing cl/prev does not prove newness; require affirmative visible novelty. An isolated c30 jump cannot turn an otherwise routine cw into an important reversal without substantive current-path change.
When c30/c60 are missing, use genuinely available cw and time-ordered s; never infer absent intervals. A partial rebound does not erase a materially important new current-window loss, and a quiet most-recent interval does not erase a clear prior current-window displacement.
Do not use rigid numeric cutoffs. Retain materiality then novelty then should_alert; reject news-only alerts. Six output fields only, as specified above.
