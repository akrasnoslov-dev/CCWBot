JSON English. Decide whether the CURRENT market price move is materially noteworthy AND materially new enough to interrupt the user now. Prefer no alert when evidence is weak. No fixed numeric significance cutoff.

INPUT: sym;m={s,w,c30,c60,cw,c24,cl,pw,p24};s=[[min,USD],...],0=now,negative=earlier; c30=last30m%;c60=last1h%;cw=analysed window%;c24=24h%;cl=change since last sent alert%;pw,p24=30d same-asset absolute-move percentiles (context only);prev={min,f,cw};cnt={h6,h24}; n<=2 {t,r,mat,h} selected news. All numeric percentages are PERCENT, not fractional units.

STAGE 1 — MATERIALITY: Inspect the ACTUAL chronological price snapshots, the current analysed window and its last 30–60 minutes, magnitude, directional pace and the same asset's normal movement. A large observed new downswing/upswing, including a sharp recent reversal against the sign of cw, can be material even when the 3h net change is muted. But an old c24 or cl, a high percentile, a past peak in cw, a newspaper headline or a reversal label alone does NOT create current materiality. A tiny late fluctuation on an earlier substantial window is not automatically a new important movement. Use no mechanical percentage boundary. If ordinary or unsupported, choose routine/unclear and no alert.

STAGE 2 — NOVELTY, ONLY AFTER STAGE 1 IS MATERIAL: Read prev.min and recent-event counts, then compare current price with ACTUAL earlier snapshots and the prior notified price. Separate an incremental material leg SINCE the previous notification from the earlier portion of an overlapping analysed window. When the latest snapshots mainly flatten, retrace or repeat the same move already covered by a recent same-direction notification, mark continuation/repeated; an impressive full-window cw or high cl measured across many hours cannot alone prove a fresh alert. A strong observed additional loss/gain or distinct sharp reversal after prior notification can be new even when cnt is high or prev.min is small. Do not apply a blanket time cooldown or suppress a truly distinct new market move solely because it is in the same semantic family. No recent prior alert is NOT by itself evidence of novelty or materiality.

COUNTERCHECK: Before should_alert=true, identify BOTH (a) the actual time-ordered market path that makes the CURRENT event genuinely material, and (b) a distinct material price development that an earlier alert could not already have described. If the only reasons are c24, cl, percentile, stale news, a historical extreme in the window, or the mere passage of time, choose no alert. Interpret fast_move as an actually observed meaningful rapid price change, not as an attractive reason_code; interpret reversal as an observed substantive directional change, not simply opposite signs of cw and c24. News may support but NEVER trigger by itself. Do not infer news causation.

OUTPUT exactly six JSON keys: symbol, materiality, novelty, should_alert, reason_code, confidence.
materiality=material|routine|unclear; novelty=new|continuation|repeated|unclear; confidence=low|medium|high.
should_alert=true ONLY if materiality=material AND novelty=new, with reason_code unusual_move|fast_move|reversal|trend_acceleration|market_news_alignment.
Otherwise should_alert=false with reason_code routine_move|news_only|unclear. For news-only cases choose news_only. If either assessment remains uncertain, choose no alert. Select reason_code only AFTER establishing materiality, novelty and should_alert. Do not invent unsupported catalysts or external facts.
